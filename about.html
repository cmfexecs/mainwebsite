Who We Are

Founded in 2023 in San Diego by Reyes Rodriguez (pka: Mr. Lil One), the Chicano Music Festival (CMF) is a fast-growing cultural platform built to celebrate, elevate, and protect the depth and diversity of Chicano culture. The CMF is designed as a living expression of identity where cross-generational audiences connect, ensuring that culture is not only preserved but aggressively pushed forward.

Operating as a traveling cultural summit with a history of large-scale events across multiple cities, the CMF forces the world to watch a culture evolve in real-time. It dismantles monolithic stereotypes by proving that the Chicano universe contains multitudes—from indigenous resilience and stoic philosophy to high-art futurism and technological innovation.

The festival experience is engineered around two distinct cultural pillars:

The Foundation: A dedicated focus on protecting and perfecting the legacy acts, traditional roots, and historical Chicano culture.

The Evolution: A forward-looking initiative focused on pushing boundaries through experimental soundscapes, intellectual tech-integrations, and high-level digital art.

Beyond the stage, the CMF is a complete, immersive cultural gathering. Each stop on the festival’s journey features a highly curated ecosystem of lowriders, live art, poetry, Aztec dancers, mariachis, and specialized vendors.

The Chicano Music Festival goes where the culture needs it. It is more than a concert; it is a reflection of who we are—past, present, and future.

Executive Team

Mr. Lil One

CEO & Founder

The visionary behind the Chicano Music Festival, Mr. Lil One is a pillar of the Chicano hip-hop community with a career spanning over 80 albums. Beyond his prolific musical contributions, he is a dedicated advocate for mental health and sobriety. His mission for the CMF is to protect the legacy of Chicano culture while empowering the next generation through hard work, community outreach, and an unwavering commitment to the Barrio.

Angelina Rodriguez

Director Of Legacy Operations

As the Director of Legacy Operations, Angelina Rodriguez manages the festival’s foundational infrastructure and day-to-day logistics. An elite operator under the age of 25, she combines a deep personal dedication to the festival’s mission with a high-efficiency approach to project management. She ensures the bedrock of the CMF remains stable, allowing for the seamless integration of new experimental activations without compromising the festival's traditional roots.

Eddie Rangel

Director Of Expansion

A veteran of the festival circuit, Eddie Rangel oversees financial modeling, logistics, and corporate partnerships. His experience in managing and overseeing complex, multi-stage festival environments informs his lean, data-driven approach to CMF’s expansion. Eddie translates abstract creative visions into scalable business models.

KILLcRey & Gene Flo

Conceptual Architects

Operating as The Invisible Panchos, this duo oversees the "Evolution" of the CMF. From building multi-dimensional narrative universes and high-art aesthetics to developing proprietary festival-mapping technology, they manage the festival’s intellectual and digital expansion. Their focus is on future-proofing the CMF by blending experimental soundscapes, immersive art, and tech-driven community service, ensuring the Chicano experience is viewed as dynamic, intellectual, and limitless.
